# Hotel-Booking-Cancellations-Dashboard (Interactive Dashboard creation using MS Excel)
## Project Objective
Interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard uncovering hotel booking trends, cancellation drivers, guest segmentation, and room assignment satisfaction across City and Resort hotels (2015–2017).


## Dataset used
- <a href="https://github.com/shrutikini872004/Hotel-Booking-Cancellations-Dashboard/blob/main/Hotel%20booking%20project.xlsx">Dataset</a>

## Questions (KPIs)
- Compare total bookings and cancelled bookings across 2015, 2016, and 2017.
- Which month got the highest bookings and cancellations?
- Which hotel type had more bookings and cancellations - City Hotel or Resort Hotel?
- What is the overall cancellation rate out of total bookings?
- Relation between guest type (Couples, Single, Family) and cancelled bookings.
- How does room assignment match (Desired vs. Un-desired) impact total bookings and cancellations?
- Which guest segment contributes the highest number of reservations?
- Percentage of total bookings cancelled vs. checked-out.

## Process
- Verify hotel booking data for any missing values, duplicates, and anomalies, and sort out the same.
- Make sure data is consistent and clean with respect to data type, date format, room status, and guest categories.
- Created pivot tables according to the questions asked (total bookings, cancellations, hotel type, guest type, room match, and monthly trends).
- Merge all pivot tables and charts into one dashboard and apply year slicer (2015–2017) to make dynamic.

## Project Insights
- Out of 119,390 total bookings, 44,224 bookings were cancelled, resulting in a 37.04% cancellation rate.
- City Hotels received significantly more bookings and cancellations compared to Resort Hotels.
- Couples accounted for the highest booking volume and cancellations, while Families had the lowest cancellation count.
- Most guests received their Desired room type, whereas Un-desired room assignments made up a very small fraction of bookings.
- Booking demand and cancellations peaked in October and dropped sharply in November and December.

## Final Conclusion
To reduce the 37.04% cancellation rate, hotel management should focus on City Hotel bookings and Couples, as they drive the highest cancellation volume. Introducing stricter cancellation policies, partial advance deposits during peak months (August–October), and targeted promotional offers in slower winter months (November–December) will help maximize room occupancy and protect revenue.
